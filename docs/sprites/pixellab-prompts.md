# PixelLab prompt list (one paid month)

Everything goes on the **same character**: the neutral base, which already has Idle, Walking and the Fighting pose. Make each one in **8 directions**.

**Two kinds of entry:**
- **Animation** (PixelLab's "Add animation"): moving, several frames.
- **State** (PixelLab's "New state"): a single held pose. Use this for poses that don't move. It's cheaper, and the game holds them.

**Rules in every prompt (don't remove these):**
- **No weapon words** (sword, blade, bow, staff…). PixelLab draws whatever is mentioned.
- **"The striking hand"** is always the character's own RIGHT hand, which is on the **viewer's LEFT** in the front (south) view. If PixelLab uses the other hand but uses it the **same in every direction**, keep it, tell me, and I'll switch the weapon hand.
- **"Keep the character, size, colours, outline and clothes exactly the same as the idle state."**

**Workflow:**
1. Make **one** animation in all 8 directions.
2. Download the zip and send it to me.
3. I check it (hand, size, feet) before you do the next one, so a problem costs one generation, not ten.
4. When everything is done, download the **final full zip** once more and send it.

## Battle (do these first)

### 1. Attack: overhead chop (animation, 5–6 frames)
```
Overhead chopping arm attack with the character's own RIGHT arm (on the viewer's LEFT in the front view). The striking hand is a closed fist the whole time, holding nothing. 1) Ready: knees slightly bent, striking fist in front of the chest. 2) Wind-up: striking fist raised high above and behind the head, body leaning back, weight on the back foot. 3) Chop: big step forward, the arm swings down and forward in a strong arc. 4) Impact: arm fully extended forward at waist height, body leaning in. 5) Recover: back to the ready stance. The other arm balances naturally. Keep the character, size, colours, outline and clothes exactly the same as the idle state. Empty hands, no effects, no motion lines.
```

### 2. Attack: forward thrust (animation, 4 frames)
```
Straight forward thrust with the character's own RIGHT arm (on the viewer's LEFT in the front view), like a fencing lunge. The striking hand is a closed fist, holding nothing. 1) Ready: striking fist pulled back at the hip, knees bent. 2) Lunge: big step forward, the arm shoots straight forward at chest height, body leaning in. 3) Hold: arm fully extended. 4) Recover: step back to the ready stance. Keep the character, size, colours, outline and clothes exactly the same as the idle state. Empty hands, no effects.
```

### 3. Hurt (animation, 3–4 frames)
```
Taking a hit: the character is struck from the front and recoils backward. 1) Impact: body jolts back, head snaps back, eyes squeezed shut, arms jerk up. 2) Stagger: one step back, leaning away, wincing. 3) Recover: back to a tense standing pose. Keep the character, size, colours, outline and clothes exactly the same as the idle state. No blood, no effects.
```

### 4. Knocked out (animation, 4 frames)
```
Falling down defeated: 1) knees buckle, 2) falls backward, 3) hits the ground, 4) lies flat on the back on the ground, eyes closed, arms loose at the sides, completely still. The whole body stays inside the canvas. Keep the character, size, colours, outline and clothes exactly the same as the idle state. No blood, no effects.
```

### 5. Kneel / low health (state)
```
Kneeling on one knee, exhausted and badly hurt: right knee on the ground, left foot flat in front, one hand resting on the raised knee, head lowered, shoulders slumped. Keep the character, size, colours, outline and clothes exactly the same as the idle state. No weapon.
```

### 6. Cast / use item (animation, 4 frames)
```
Casting a spell with both hands, no objects: 1) both hands together in front of the chest, eyes closed, focusing. 2) Hands pull apart slightly, palms facing each other. 3) Both arms thrust up and forward, palms open toward the target. 4) Arms lower back to a relaxed stance. Keep the character, size, colours, outline and clothes exactly the same as the idle state. No magic effects, no glow, no objects.
```

### 7. Defend / guard (state)
```
Defensive guard: feet apart, knees bent, both forearms raised and crossed in front of the face, bracing for a hit, eyes narrowed. Keep the character, size, colours, outline and clothes exactly the same as the idle state. Empty hands, no shield.

### 8. Dodge (animation, 3 frames)
```
Quick dodge backward: 1) leans away, 2) hops one step backward with both feet off the ground, 3) lands in a crouched ready stance. Keep the character, size, colours, outline and clothes exactly the same as the idle state. No effects.

- **Cut off at the edges:** add "**the whole body stays inside the canvas**".
