# Card game lore

Card game lore The lore inside the card game, as you wrote it in your story notes (largely incomplete). In the video game, Adventure Scuffle! is a card game based on a hit TV anime and video game series about Misha, so this is the anime's world, not the player's; the video game's own story is in Adventure Scuffle: game roadmap. New decks come from new regions of the Age of Magic; their lore is yours to write.

Setting Set in the Yfera timeline, in the Age of Magic, before Yfera awakens (late medieval period). You want to write a story in this era based on the game.

The base set: The First Quest The border town of Rotos is the focus. Three factions are in conflict, and a fourth party quietly resolves it. Adventurer's Guild: bounty hunters pursuing the Necromancer of Rotos. Hero's Retinue: the Rianz Church's chosen hero and followers, who are also hunting her. Necromancer Deck: the misunderstood "villain." She has her family's consent to reanimate the dead and creatures, and is the true protector of Rotos against the Banded Horde. Unassuming Mage (TFQ-37): secretly resolved the Guild vs Hero vs Necromancer conflict. The card represents Misha, which is why it got the unique Guardian Ability treatment.

Misha of Toadfoot Village An unassuming Mage from Toadfoot Village. He is distinct from the space-age Misha Revali, who is named after him. His wand is three coiled elder-tree branches that unravel into a sword of pure golden light.

He secretly fights incursion beings (the souls of dead planets and stars) while joining adventuring parties as cover. He is extremely humble and self-effacing. He would never give his own effort a grandiose name, which is why his deck is "Anti-Incursion" and not "Dawnwardens." Only planets with life, or stars, have a soul. Misha was chosen by the Sun (Yfera's parent star), and the wand holds a fraction of its power. He is effectively immortal and the world's guardian. In the ending of Battleline Requiem, he secretly captures a fragment of Yfera after her death as Atasha. He marries Hannah ri Aldonica and has two children. She is the progenitor of House Aldonica (Celine's house). House Aldonica's founders, Teren Aldonica and Lysanne Nartes, raised the barrier that saved the archipelago later named Nartes. A House Nartes member became its ruler about 500 years before Battleline Requiem, with House Aldonica's support throughout.

Charge of the Horde (deferred expansion) The Banded Horde (Orcs, Goblins, Kobolds, Sahuagin) is besieging Rotos, but is secretly fleeing a worse threat. (Remade as The United Horde, Misha's ally; see the story by set.) The Creeping Dread is what they are fleeing, a Voidspawn-type antagonist. Deepdelver, a small Dwarven Party (3 Bonus Cards), accidentally opened the chamber that released it.

Power Unbound The Unassuming Mage's true form is the Sunlight Guardian. His power no longer needs "opponent has an Ace" to trigger, and that condition would not apply against Fear anyway because Fear has no Ace. Anti-Incursion is his deck. It was renamed from "The Dawnwardens" because that name was too grandiose for his humble personality. Fear is the actual incursion at the heart of everything. It first appeared as a Solo-only Boss Deck of 12 body-part cards (tentacles, eyes, claws, head and so on). It is now playable as Fear Descends, with Fear Incarnate as its Ace.

Set 1, The First Quest. The Kingdom of Rianz and the Adventurer's Guild both believe the Necromancer of Rotos is an evil mastermind, and send their best to defeat her. In truth she uses her power to defend Rotos from the United Horde, whose races have united for reasons no one yet knows and are rampaging toward the town. The Hero of Rianz and the Adventurer's Guild meet on the road and are hostile from the start: the adventurers fear losing their share of the reward, and the Hero sees adventurers as mercenaries, even though their leader is a noblewoman, Hannah ri Aldonica of House Aldonica, the Crimson Duelist. Through a series of wild events Misha stops the three-way conflict and gets the factions to work together against the Horde. This is Hannah and Misha's first meeting. On his way to the Horde's camp Misha meets a scout of an incursion, Fear (Fear the First Shadow), and promptly defeats it. Hannah sees it and becomes intrigued by who Misha really is.

Set 2, The United Front. Misha meets the battered Horde and convinces it to stand down from attacking Rotos. He learns its races are fleeing what they call the Creeping Dread, and that they blame the Dwarves for releasing it on purpose. The Warchief challenges Misha to prove his strength; Misha wins, and the Horde follows him to investigate. In the mountains the Deepdelver Dwarves, knowing the Horde blames them, attack first, and a brief battle breaks out until Misha unleashes part of his power to stop it. In a tense meeting both camps learn the Dwarves released the Dread by accident while mining. On an expedition with the Dwarves, Misha learns the Creeping Dread are guardians of a beacon calling an incursion entity. The Horde and the Dwarves, with Misha's help, defeat the Creeping Dread, but too late: Fear has arrived. Set 3, The Light of Courage. Misha unleashes his full power, as the Sunlight Guardian, and confronts Fear Incarnate. The Falconwatch only get involved because they happen to see Fear descend, and are inexplicably drawn into the fight. Set 4, The Arms of Creation. Story not mapped yet. Its bonus cards include three Aces: Guardian of Creation (Misha's third and final form), Crimson Blademeister (Hannah's second card) and Determined Hero (the Hero of Rianz's second card). Set 5, The Entropy Cometh. Story not mapped yet. The final evil deck is Entropy-themed: the last enemy of Misha's story. Misha's three forms: Unassuming Mage (The First Quest, a bonus Ace), the Sunlight Guardian (Anti-Incursion's Ace, The Light of Courage), and Guardian of Creation (The Arms of Creation, a special bonus Ace, 4/3/3, no ability except that it can be a deck's second Ace; the only card with 4 Fighting Spirit). Allies: the United Horde is Misha's ally. Hannah ri Aldonica is the Crimson Duelist; she later marries Misha (see above).
