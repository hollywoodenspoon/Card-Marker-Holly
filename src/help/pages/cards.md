# Cards list and editing many cards

Every card in your project is listed in the **Cards** panel. From there you add, find, reorder and rename cards, and you can change a whole batch of cards in one go.

## The Cards list

On desktop, press **Cards** in the rail on the left. On a tablet it is in the left drawer, and on a phone it is **Cards** in the bottom bar.

Each row shows a small front and back picture of the card and its name. Click a row to open that card on the canvas.

![The Cards list with front and back thumbnails](cards.webp)

## Adding cards

- **+ Card** makes a new card right after the one you have open. It uses the same front and back templates and the same parts turned on or off, but its fields are empty, so it shows the template's default text and pictures.
- The small arrow next to **+ Card** has more choices:
  - **New card using previous card's layout**: the same as **+ Card**.
  - **New card with default toggles**: the same templates, but every part shown or hidden the way the template says.
  - **Mail merge from spreadsheet…**: makes many cards at once from a spreadsheet. See [Mail merge from a spreadsheet](help:mail-merge).

New cards are named _Card 1_, _Card 2_ and so on. Rename them whenever you like.

## Renaming, duplicating and deleting

Press **⋯** on a card's row:

- **Rename** (or double-click the name). Press [[Enter]] to keep the new name, [[Esc]] to cancel.
- **Duplicate** makes an exact copy, named "… copy", with its own text, pictures, part toggles and card-only layers.
- **Export as image…** saves a picture of the card. See [Card images](help:card-images).
- **Delete** removes the card straight away. A message appears with **Undo** in case you didn't mean it.

## Searching and reordering

Type in **Search** to show only cards whose name contains those letters.

To change the order, drag a card by the grip on the left of its row. The grips are hidden while you search or select, so clear the search box first. The order matters: card numbers such as `{#}` count the card's place in this list (see [Keywords, card numbers and symbols](help:keywords-numbers)).

## Changing many cards at once

1. Press **Select** at the top of the list. Tick boxes appear.
2. Tap cards to tick them, or press **All** (every card shown, so after a search it picks only the matches) or **None**.
3. Use the bar at the bottom. It shows how many cards are selected and has:
   - **Front template** and **Back template**: open **Change…** and pick a template. The back list also has **No back**.
   - **Template part**: choose a part, then press the eye to show it or the crossed-out eye to hide it on all the selected cards. Parts the template marks as always shown are not listed.
   - **Print copies**: type a number (0 to 99) and press **Set**.
   - The copy button duplicates the selected cards.
   - The bin button deletes them. Press **Delete** again to confirm.
4. Press **Done** to leave select mode.

Each change is one step you can undo with [[Ctrl+Z]] (⌘ on Mac) or the undo button in the top bar.

> **Tip:** To change every card with "Dragon" in its name, search for _Dragon_, press **Select**, then **All**.

## Changing values on many cards

To change text, art or icon counts on lots of cards, the [card data table](help:card-data) shows every card's values side by side. To swap a word everywhere, use [Find and replace](help:find-replace).
