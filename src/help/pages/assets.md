# Assets and folders

Assets are the pictures you upload to a project. They are kept as the original files, at full quality, and every card and template in the project can use them.

## Where Assets are

- **Desktop and tablet:** at the bottom of the left pane. Click the **Assets** header to fold the section away or open it again; this is remembered on this device. The number next to the header is how many images you have.
- **Phone:** **Assets** in the bottom bar opens them as a sheet.

![The Assets section with folders](assets.webp)

## Uploading

- Press the **Upload images** button (the arrow in the Assets header). You can pick several files at once.
- Or drop picture files onto the canvas, or paste one with [[Ctrl+V]] (⌘ on Mac). These are added to Assets and placed on the card.

PNG, JPG, WebP and SVG work. In the desktop app, uploaded pictures are saved unchanged in the project folder's `images` folder.

The pen button next to the upload button opens the Vector Maker, where you can draw your own shapes and icons. See [Drawing shapes](help:vector-basics).

## Using an asset

Click (or tap) an image to put it on the card. What happens depends on what is selected:

- An **art box** is selected: the picture fills it, for this card (or as default art in template mode).
- An **icon row** is selected (in template mode): the picture becomes its icon.
- Anything else: the picture is added as a new image in the middle of the card.

## Image options

Each image has a ⋯ button:

- **Rename** changes the name shown under the tile.
- **Symbol name…** lets you type `{name}` in text to show this image inline. See [inline symbols](help:keywords-numbers#inline-symbols). The name then shows on the tile.
- **Move to folder…** puts it in a folder, takes it out (**No folder**), or makes a new folder (**Create and move**).
- **Edit in Vector Maker** appears for SVGs and drawings, and opens them for editing.
- **Delete** removes it from the project.

## Folders

Folders keep big projects tidy, e.g. "Icons", "Frames", "Art".

1. Press **New folder** (the folder button in the Assets header), type a name and press **Create**.
2. Drag an image onto the folder's name (with a mouse or pen), or use ⋯ → **Move to folder…** (this also works on touch).

Click a folder's name to open or close it. The folder's own ⋯ has **Upload here**, **Rename** and **Delete folder (keeps images)**: the images move back out of the folder, nothing is deleted.

## Deleting images

**Delete** asks first. If the image is used on a card or template, the message says so; those places will show an empty box after you delete it.

> **Note:** Deleting an image is not part of undo. If you might need it again, move it to a folder such as "Old" instead.
