# Decks

A deck says how many copies of each card it holds, for example a starter deck for each player. A card can be in as many decks as you like, and deleting a deck never deletes the cards.

Deck counts are separate from the **print copies** in the Print view, so you can keep a "print everything once" list and your playable decks side by side.

## Opening the Decks panel

On desktop, press **Decks** in the rail on the left. On a tablet it is in the left drawer. On a phone, open the ☰ menu and choose **Decks**.

## Making a deck

1. Press **New deck**. If you already have decks, use the **+** button next to the deck list.
2. Every card in the project is listed. Press **+** to add a copy of a card to the deck and **−** to take one out. A card can be in a deck 0 to 99 times. Cards at 0 are faded.
3. The line under the deck name shows the total, for example _40 cards · 18 different_.

Use the **Search** box to find a card by name in a long list.

![The Decks panel with counts for each card](decks.webp)

The deck list at the top shows each deck's name and total. Pick another deck there to edit it.

## Renaming, duplicating and deleting

Press **⋯** next to the deck list:

- **Rename**: type the new name and press [[Enter]].
- **Duplicate**: a copy with the same counts, named "… copy". Handy for making a second player's deck that differs by a few cards.
- **Delete deck**: removes the deck (not its cards). A message appears with **Undo**.

## Printing a deck

Press **Print deck**. The Print view opens with that deck chosen, and each card is printed as many times as the deck holds it.

In the Print view, the **Print** list at the top of the cards section chooses what to print:

- **Cards to print (this list)**: the normal print list, with its own ticks and copies.
- **Deck: …**: one of your decks. The view then says how many cards and sheets it will print. To change the counts, go back to the Decks panel.

The **Print** list only appears once the project has at least one deck. See [Printing on paper](help:printing) for the rest of the print settings.

## Other places that use decks

- **Gallery**: the deck filter shows only that deck's cards, with how many of each.
- **Card data**: the deck filter shows only that deck's cards, and **Export CSV** saves just those rows.
- **Tabletop Simulator**: pick a deck to export as deck sheets.

> **Tip:** Make one deck per player or per product (a starter, a booster), then print or export whichever you need without touching the print list.
