# The workspace

The editor looks a little different on a computer, a tablet and a phone, but it has the same parts everywhere: the top bar, the panels, the canvas with your card, and the properties for whatever you've selected.

![The editor on a computer](editor.webp)

## Top bar

From left to right:

- **Project name**: click it to rename the project.
- **Save indicator**: _Saved_, _Saving…_ or _Not saved_.
- **Card / Template** switch: fill in a card, or change the shared layout. See [Templates and fields](help:templates).
- **Add**: Text, Image…, Image field (art box), Icon row (stat), Card number, Rectangle, Ellipse, Line.
- **Print**: opens the print preview and PDF export.
- **Undo** and **Redo**.
- **☰ menu**, at the right end: Find and replace…, Export for Tabletop Simulator…, Export backup file, All projects (and **Show project folder** in the desktop app), and **Settings**. On a phone, ☰ also has everything the rail has on a computer (Print, Gallery, Playtest, Templates, Decks, Fonts, Export card images, Keywords, Vector Maker and Card data).
- **Settings** (inside ☰): Help, Units…, Theme… and, in the apps, the update and install items. Help stays here on every screen size.

The ☰ menu sits at the top right everywhere, on the Home screen too.

## Left pane

On a computer the left pane has a narrow rail of buttons. The top four switch the panel next to the rail:

- **Templates**: your layouts for fronts and backs.
- **Cards**: every card, with search, reorder and multi-select.
- **Decks**: groups of cards with counts.
- **Fonts**: upload and manage fonts.

**Templates** and **Cards** also switch the mode: the Templates tab goes to **Template** mode on the current card's template, and the Cards tab goes back to **Card** mode. Switching the mode with the **Card / Template** switch moves the tab the same way. **Decks** and **Fonts** leave the mode as it is.

Below a line come quick buttons that open full-screen views: **Print**, **Gallery**, **Export** (card images), **Keywords**, **Vector** (the Vector Maker), **Data** (card table, CSV and mail merge) and **Help**.

**Assets** sit at the bottom of the left pane. Click the **Assets** header to fold them away or open them again. The header buttons draw a new vector, make a folder, or upload images.

## Canvas

The card sits in the middle on a neutral grey background, with rulers along the top and left.

- The **Front / Back** switch above the card shows either side.
- In Template mode, a banner says which template you're editing and how many cards use it, with **Back to card**.
- The buttons at the bottom right show or hide the bleed and safe area, turn snapping on or off, and zoom.

See [Canvas: zoom, pan, snapping and undo](help:canvas).

## Right pane

- **Layers** at the top lists every part of the card. Click the header to fold it. See [Layers, groups and alignment](help:layers).
- **Properties** below changes with the selection. With nothing selected it shows the card size, units, bleed, safe margin, corner guide and card numbers. With a part selected, its name is at the top, then its own settings (text, image, icon row…) and **Layout** (position, size, rotation, flip, opacity, arrange).

## Tablet

On a tablet the panels slide out as drawers so the card gets the room:

- **Open panels** (left end of the top bar) opens the rail, panels and Assets.
- **Open properties** (right end) opens Layers and Properties.

## Phone

![The editor on a phone](editor-phone.webp)

On a phone the card fills the screen:

- The top bar keeps ☰, the project name, the save icon, **Undo** and **Redo**.
- The **Card / Template** switch sits above the card, next to **Front / Back**.
- The bottom bar has **Cards**, **Layers**, **Add**, **Assets** and **Edit** (properties). Each opens a sheet from the bottom.
- Close a sheet with ✕, by tapping its button again, or by swiping its header down.
- The **Edit** sheet stays low, so you can still see and tap the card above it.
- When you edit text, a panel opens at the top of the screen with the text tools and a **Done** button.
- **Templates**, **Decks**, **Fonts**, **Print / export PDF** and the other rail views are in the ☰ menu.
