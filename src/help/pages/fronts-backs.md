# Fronts and backs

Every card has two sides, and each side has its own template. Usually all cards share one back, but you can give different cards different backs.

A new project comes with two templates: "Basic front" and "Basic back".

## Switching sides

In **Card** mode, the **Front** / **Back** switch sits above the canvas. Press **Back** to see and design the back of the current card. Everything works the same as on the front: add text, art boxes and shapes, hide parts, and so on.

In template mode the switch is hidden, because a template is either a front or a back. The bar above the card says which side you're editing.

## Front and back templates

The **Templates** tab lists **Fronts** and **Backs** separately (the side you're looking at comes first). Press **+** next to **Backs** to make a new back template; it starts with a white background to build on. A back template is edited like any other, see [Templates and fields](help:templates).

## Choosing a card's back

1. Open the **Templates** tab (on a phone, ☰ → **Templates**).
2. Click a back template, or use its ⋯ → **Use for this card**.

The editor switches to the back so you can see it. A tick marks the templates the current card uses.

## One back for the whole deck

On a back template, press ⋯ → **Use as back for all cards**. Every card now uses that back. Edit it once, and every card's back changes.

New cards made with **+ Card** use the same templates as the current card, so they keep the shared back.

## Different backs

Want a separate back for, say, the encounter deck? Make a second back template, then give it to those cards:

1. In the **Cards** tab, press **Select** and tick the cards.
2. In the bar at the bottom, choose the template under **Back template**.

The same menu has **No back** for cards that don't need one. A side with no template shows "This side has no template. Pick one in the Templates panel."

## Fields on the back

Backs can have fields too, such as a deck name or a card number (`{#}`). Fields are matched by name across the whole card.

> **Tip:** A back text field with the same field name as a front field, such as "Title", shows the same words as the front. Give back fields their own names if they should differ.

## Printing both sides

When you print, the backs sheet is mirrored so each back lands behind its front when you print double-sided. See [Double-sided printing](help:double-sided) to set the flip and print an alignment test.
