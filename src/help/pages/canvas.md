# Canvas: zoom, pan, snapping and undo

The canvas shows your card at its real size in mm, with rulers, guides and snapping to help you place things exactly.

![Zoomed in on the canvas](canvas-zoom.webp)

## Zoom

- The buttons at the bottom right: **−**, **+**, and **Fit to screen**. The percentage between them also fits the card to the screen when you click it.
- [[Ctrl]] + mouse wheel zooms in and out at the mouse pointer (⌘ on Mac).
- [[Ctrl+=]] zooms in, [[Ctrl+-]] zooms out, [[Ctrl+0]] fits the card to the screen.
- On a touch screen, pinch with two fingers.

You can zoom from 10% to 1600%. At 100% the card is shown at about its real size on a typical screen.

## Pan

- Turn the mouse wheel, or swipe on a touchpad, to move around.
- Hold [[Space]] and drag, or drag with the middle mouse button.
- On a touch screen, drag with two fingers.

## Select and move

- Click a part to select it. [[Shift]]-click or [[Ctrl]]-click adds or removes parts.
- With a mouse, drag a box on an empty area to select everything it touches.
- [[Ctrl+A]] selects every part on this side that isn't locked. [[Esc]] clears the selection.
- Click empty space to select nothing.
- Drag to move. The arrow keys nudge by 0.5 mm, or 5 mm with [[Shift]].
- Clicking inside an already-selected group selects the part you clicked.

In Card mode, the template's parts stay where the template puts them. You can select them to fill in text or art, but to move or resize them, switch to **Template** mode.

## Resize and rotate

- Drag a corner or edge handle to resize. Pictures keep their proportions; hold [[Shift]] to resize freely. Other parts resize freely; hold [[Shift]] to keep their proportions.
- Hold [[Alt]] while resizing to resize from the centre.
- Drag the round handle above the selection to rotate. It clicks into 45° steps when you come close to one; hold [[Shift]] to turn in 15° steps.
- For exact numbers, type **X**, **Y**, **Width**, **Height** and **Rotation** in the **Layout** section of the right pane.

## Snapping

While you drag, parts snap to:

- the card's edges and centre lines
- the bleed edge and the safe area
- your guides
- the edges and centres of the other parts

Blue lines show what you snapped to. The magnet button at the bottom right turns snapping off or on.

## Rulers and guides

The rulers along the top and left are in your chosen units (see [Card size, bleed and safe area](help:card-size#units)).

- To make a **guide**, drag from a ruler onto the card.
- To move a guide, drag it.
- To remove a guide, drag it back onto the ruler.

Guides belong to the template, so every card using that template shows them. They're never printed.

## Bleed and safe area

The first button in the controls at the bottom right shows or hides the bleed area and safe-area lines. See [Card size, bleed and safe area](help:card-size#bleed).

## Undo and redo

Everything you change can be undone: text, moves, template edits, card changes, deleting cards and more.

- **Undo** and **Redo** in the top bar.
- [[Ctrl+Z]] undoes. [[Ctrl+Shift+Z]] or [[Ctrl+Y]] redoes.

A whole drag, or a burst of typing in one box, counts as one step. The last 200 steps are kept while the project is open; opening another project starts afresh.

When you delete layers, cards or decks, they go straight away and a message appears with an **Undo** button.

> **Tip:** While you're typing inside a text, [[Ctrl+Z]] undoes your typing in that text. After you finish editing, it undoes whole changes again.
