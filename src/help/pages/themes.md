# Themes

A theme sets the colours of the app around your cards. It never changes the cards themselves: the canvas behind the card stays neutral grey in every theme, so your card's colours always look true.

## Choosing a theme

1. Open ☰ at the top right and choose **Settings** → **Theme…**.
2. Click a theme. It applies straight away; close the window when you like what you see.

![The theme picker](theme-picker.webp)

The theme is remembered on this device. Hearthside Table, the playtest app, uses the same theme.

## The themes

- **Basics**: **Light**, **Dark** and **Follow system** (light or dark to match your device).
- **Hearth**: **Hearthside** (a moonlit night with candle glow, the theme new users start with), **Ember**, **Roaring fire** and **Golden candlelight**.
- **Jobs**: **Red Mage**, **Black Mage**, **White Mage**, **Time Mage**, **Dragoon**, **Paladin**, **Dark Knight**, **Monk**, **Summoner**, **Ninja**, **Bard** and **Thief**.
- **Fun**: **Bubblegum pop**, **Midnight arcade**, **Forest**, **Sunset**, **Chocobo** and **Gold Chocobo**.
- **Tabletop**: **Parchment and ink**, **Dungeon stone**, **Royal purple and gold** and **Tavern wood**.
- **Pastels**: **Lavender**, **Mint**, **Peach** and **Sky**.
- **Bold darks**: **Ocean night**, **Volcano**, **Neon city** and **Deep forest**.
- **Accessible**: **High contrast (light)**, **High contrast (dark)** and **Colour-blind friendly** (blue and orange instead of red and green).

Every theme is checked for readable text: body text has a contrast of at least 7:1 against the background, and smaller text at least 4.5:1.
