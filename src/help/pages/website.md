# Website and offline use

You can use Hearthside Cardworks straight from your web browser at [hollywoodenspoon.github.io/Card-Marker-Holly](https://hollywoodenspoon.github.io/Card-Marker-Holly/). Nothing to install, no account, and nothing is sent anywhere: your work stays on your own device.

## Where the website keeps your work

On the website, projects, pictures and fonts are stored **inside the browser** you use. That means:

- They're only in **that browser on that device**. Chrome and Firefox on the same computer each have their own projects, and so do your laptop and your phone.
- If you clear the browser's data for this site (for example "Clear browsing data" with site data or cookies ticked), your projects are deleted with it.
- A private or incognito window forgets everything when you close it.

To keep your work safe, export a backup file from time to time: ☰ → **Export backup file**. To move work to another browser, another device or the desktop app, export a backup there and **Import** it on the Home screen of the other one. See [Projects, saving and backups](help:projects).

> **Tip:** If you'd rather have ordinary files you can see and copy, use the [Desktop app](help:desktop-app) or the [Android app](help:android-app). Both open your backup files.

## Downloads

PDFs, card images and other exports are normal browser downloads. They go wherever your browser saves downloads, usually the **Downloads** folder.

## Install it as an app

Most browsers can install the website as an app with its own window and icon:

1. Open ☰ at the top right (in the editor or on the Home screen).
2. Choose **Settings** → **Install app**.

**Install app** only shows when your browser offers it. If you don't see it, look for the browser's own install option (often in its address bar or menu, or **Add to Home screen** on a phone). An installed app keeps using the same browser storage, so your projects are the same in both.

## Working offline

After your first visit, Hearthside Cardworks works without an internet connection, including PDF export. A message says **Ready to work offline.** when it's ready.

When a newer version is available, a message says **A new version is available.** Press **Update** to switch to it. Your projects aren't affected.

## Browser tips

- A current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari works best.
- The app has its own zoom ([[Ctrl]] and the mouse wheel, or pinch). Leave the browser's page zoom at 100% so the panels look right.
- The canvas around the card stays neutral grey in every theme, so card colours look true.
