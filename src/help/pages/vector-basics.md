# Drawing shapes

The Vector Maker is a drawing board for your own icons, symbols, frames and badges. What you draw is saved into your assets, so you can put it on cards, use it as an inline symbol or print it razor sharp.

## Opening the Vector Maker

- On desktop, press **Vector** in the rail on the left.
- Open the ☰ menu and choose **Vector Maker**.
- Press the pen button (**New vector**) in the Assets header.
- To change a drawing you saved before, open its **⋯** in Assets and choose **Edit in Vector Maker**.

Press the back arrow at the top left to return to your cards. Nothing is lost: an unsaved drawing is kept as a draft (see [Saving drawings](help:vector-save)).

## The screen

- **Tools** run down the left side. On a phone they are a row along the bottom.
- The **artboard** (the white area) is in the middle.
- The **right pane** shows the settings of what you selected, or of the artboard when nothing is selected, and **Layers** below. On a tablet or phone, open them with the **Properties** and **Layers** buttons in the top bar.

## The artboard

The artboard is measured in pixels and starts at 512 × 512. With nothing selected, the right pane lets you type a **Width** and **Height**, pick 256 × 256, 512 × 512 or 1024 × 1024, or press **Fit to artwork** to shrink it around what you drew.

Under **Snapping**, **Snap to artboard and points** (on by default) and **Snap to whole pixels** help you line things up.

## Drawing a ready-made shape

1. Press **Shapes** ([[U]]). A picker opens with 31 shapes in three groups: **Basic** (rectangles, ellipse, polygon, star, frame, ring…), **Symbols** (heart, gem, shield, flame, sword, crown, gear, badge, speech bubble, banner…) and **Arrows**.
2. Pick one, then drag on the artboard. Hold [[Shift]] for equal sides, or [[Alt]] (Option on Mac) to draw from the centre. A single click makes a 100 px shape.

Some shapes have settings, such as a star's **Points** or a rounded rectangle's **Corner radius %**. Change them in the right pane. Once you edit a shape's points, it becomes an ordinary path and the settings go away.

To draw shapes of your own, use [the pen tool](help:vector-pen).

## Selecting and moving

- **Selection** ([[V]]): click a shape to select it; [[Shift]]-click to add or remove shapes; drag a box over empty space to select everything it touches. Drag to move (hold [[Shift]] to move only straight across or up and down). Drag the corner handles to resize ([[Shift]] keeps the proportions) and the round handle to rotate.
- **Direct selection** ([[A]]): works on single points. See [Editing points afterwards](help:vector-pen#editing-points-afterwards).

## Colour, stroke and size

With a shape selected, the right pane has:

- **Fill** and **Stroke** colours (either can be none), **Stroke width** and **Opacity**.
- **Corners** (Sharp, Round, Bevel) and **Line ends** (Flat, Round, Square) for the stroke.
- **X**, **Y**, **W** and **H** for exact position and size in pixels.

New shapes take the colours you used last.

## Arrange and align

- The four arrange buttons bring the selection to the front, forward, backward or to the back.
- The six align buttons line objects up with each other. With one object selected, they align it to the artboard (for example, centre it).
- **Layer** moves the selection to another layer.
- **Path** has **Duplicate**, **Reverse direction** and **Delete**.

## Layers

New drawings start with _Layer 1_. Press **+** in Layers to add one. Click a layer's name to draw on it; click it again to rename it. Drag the grip to reorder, and use the eye and lock buttons to hide or lock a layer. The active layer has an **Opacity** slider. Open a layer to see its objects: click one to select it (again to rename), or hide and lock it on its own.

## Zoom and pan

- Hold [[Space]] and drag, use the **Hand** tool ([[H]]), the middle mouse button, or the mouse wheel to pan.
- [[Ctrl]] (⌘ on Mac) plus the wheel, the zoom buttons, or a pinch on touch screens zoom. [[Ctrl+0]] fits the artboard.

## Keyboard shortcuts

- [[Ctrl+Z]] undo, [[Ctrl+Shift+Z]] or [[Ctrl+Y]] redo
- [[Ctrl+D]] duplicate, [[Delete]] delete, [[Ctrl+A]] select all
- Arrow keys nudge 1 px ([[Shift]] for 10 px)
- [[Ctrl+S]] save
- Tools: [[V]], [[A]], [[P]], [[+]], [[-]], [[Shift+C]], [[U]], [[H]]
