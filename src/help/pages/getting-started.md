# Getting started

Card Maker is a card editor for your own card game. You design a layout once, fill in many cards, and print them on paper sheets with cut marks, ready to cut by hand. Everything saves by itself.

This page gets you from nothing to a printed card in a few minutes.

![The Home screen with your projects](home.webp)

## Make your first card

1. On the Home screen, press **Start a new project** (or **New project** if you already have some).
2. In the **New project** box, type a **Name**, then pick your **Units**, a **Card size** and **Portrait** or **Landscape**. The ID card (54 × 86 mm) is chosen for you. Leave **Bleed** at 3 mm unless you know you need something else.
3. Press **Create**. You get a ready-made front and back, and a first card called _Card 1_.
4. Double-click the words **Card name** on the card and type a name. Press [[Esc]] or click somewhere else when you're done.
5. Double-click the grey art box and pick a picture from your computer.
6. Press **Print** in the top bar, then **Export PDF**.

That's it: you have a PDF with your card, its back, and cut marks.

![The New project box](new-project.webp)

> **Tip:** On a phone or tablet, double-tap instead of double-click. On a phone, **Print** is in the ☰ menu as **Print / export PDF**.

## The first steps checklist

A small **Make your first card** box sits in the corner of the canvas in a new project. It ticks off the three steps above as you do them. Close it with ✕ whenever you like; it won't come back on this device.

## What you got

A new project starts with two templates and one card:

- **Basic front**: a background, an **Art** box, a **Title** and a **Rules** text.
- **Basic back**: a background, a border and a title.

A _template_ is the layout that many cards share. Each card fills in its own text and pictures. Change the template once and every card using it changes too. See [Templates and fields](help:templates).

## Card mode and Template mode

The **Card / Template** switch in the top bar (above the canvas on a phone) decides what you're editing:

- **Card**: you fill in this card's text and art.
- **Template**: you change the layout shared by every card that uses it. A banner above the card says which template you're editing and how many cards use it. Press **Back to card** (on a phone, **Done**) to return.

The **Front / Back** switch above the card shows either side.

## Add more cards

Open **Cards** (left pane, or the bottom bar on a phone) and press **+ Card**. The new card uses the same templates as the card you're on, so you only fill in the new text and art.
