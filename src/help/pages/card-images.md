# Card images

Besides the PDF, you can save cards as picture files: to share them online, send them to a print shop that wants single images, or use them in other programs.

## Opening the export

- **Export ▾** in the top bar → **Card images (PNG, JPG, WebP)…**
- on a phone, ☰ → **Export card images…**
- in the **Cards** list, a card's ⋯ → **Export as image…**

![The Export card images dialog](card-images.webp)

## The options

- **Cards**: the card you have open, or **All cards**. This choice appears when a card is open.
- **Sides**: **Front**, **Back**, or both.
- **Size**: **Without bleed** (just the finished card), **With 3 mm bleed** (your project's bleed), or both. The pixel size of each is shown next to it.
- **Formats**: **PNG** and **WebP** keep transparency, **JPG** has a white background. You can tick more than one.
- **Resolution**: **150 dpi**, **300 dpi** or **600 dpi**. 300 dpi is good for printing; 150 dpi is enough for screens.

The line at the bottom counts the files, for example "8 files · saved as one zip". Press **Export**.

Your choices are remembered until you close the app, so a second export starts where the last one left off.

## Sizes and resolution

Images are exactly the card's size at the chosen resolution. A 54 × 86 mm card at 300 dpi is 638 × 1016 pixels, or 708 × 1086 with 3 mm bleed. PNG and JPG files also store their resolution, so a photo or print program opens them at their real size in millimetres.

The bleed is filled the same way as in the PDF, using the **Bleed fill** setting in the print view (**Mirror edges**, **Stretch edges** or **As designed**).

## One file or a zip

- One card, one side, one size and one format gives a single image.
- Anything more is saved as one zip file named after your project, for example "My game - card images.zip".

With several cards, file names start with the card's place in the Cards list, so they sort in order, for example "01 Fire Drake - front.png" and "01 Fire Drake - back (bleed).png".

> **Note:** Cards without a back template get no back image. A message lists them after the export.
