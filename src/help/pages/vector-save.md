# Saving drawings

Drawings from the Vector Maker are saved into your project's assets, where you can place them on cards like any uploaded image. You can also download them as files for other programs.

## Saving to your assets

1. Press **Save** in the top bar, or [[Ctrl+S]] (⌘ on Mac).
2. Stay on the **Save to assets** tab.
3. Type a **Name**.
4. Pick a **Format**:
   - **SVG (vector: sharp at any size, stays vector in the PDF)**: the best choice for icons, frames and symbols.
   - **PNG (transparent background)**: a picture made of pixels, with a transparent background.
   - **JPEG**: a picture with a solid **JPEG background** colour.
     For PNG and JPEG, also pick a **Size**: 1×, 2× or 4× the artboard (the pixel size is shown).
5. Pick a **Folder**, **No folder**, or **New folder…**.
6. Press **Save to assets**.

![The Save window](vector-save.webp)

The drawing appears in Assets. Click it to put it on the card, or into a selected art box. To use it as an inline symbol in text, give it a symbol name (see [Keywords, card numbers and symbols](help:keywords-numbers)).

## Editing a saved drawing

Your layers are kept with the asset, whichever format you chose. To change the drawing later:

1. In Assets, press **⋯** on the drawing and choose **Edit in Vector Maker**.
2. Make your changes and press **Save**.
3. Leave **Replace "…" (every card using it updates)** ticked. The asset keeps its name and folder, and every card that uses it shows the new version.

Untick **Replace** to save a separate new asset and leave the old one as it was.

Uploaded SVG files have **Edit in Vector Maker** too. Paths and basic shapes come in with their colours, each top-level group as a layer. Text, gradients, masks, filters and embedded pictures in the SVG are left out.

> **Tip:** The folder button in the Vector Maker's top bar (**Open SVG file**, in the **⋯** menu on a phone) opens an SVG from your computer to edit, without uploading it first.

## Downloading a file

On the **Download** tab, type a **Name**, then press one of:

- **SVG** or **PDF**: stay vector, and open in Illustrator, Inkscape and Affinity.
- **PNG** or **JPEG**: pictures at the chosen **Size**. JPEG uses the **JPEG background** colour.

In the desktop app a normal save window opens; on the website the file downloads.

## Drafts: nothing is lost

While you work, the Vector Maker keeps your unsaved drawing as a draft on this device, one per project.

- Leave the Vector Maker with the back arrow at any time. A message says the drawing is kept as a draft.
- Next time you open the Vector Maker, a bar says _You have an unsaved drawing from …_. Press **Carry on with it** to continue, or **Discard** to throw it away.
- When you save to assets, the draft is cleared.

The draft is a safety net, not a save. It lives only in this browser or app on this device, and isn't part of your project or backup files. Save to assets to keep your drawing for good.

## SVG or PNG?

- Use **SVG** for anything printed on cards. It stays sharp at any size and stays vector in the PDF.
- Use **PNG** when another program needs a plain picture.
- Use **JPEG** only when you need a solid background and a small file.
