# Gallery and proofreading

The gallery shows all your cards at once, big enough to compare them side by side. Its **Proofread** tab lists all the text on your cards, so you can check the spelling before printing.

## Opening the gallery

- **Desktop and tablet:** press **Gallery** in the left rail.
- **Phone:** ☰ → **Gallery and proofreading**.

Press [[Esc]] or the back arrow to return to the editor.

## Looking at your cards

The **Cards** tab shows every card in Cards-list order. The bar at the top has:

- **Search**: shows only cards whose name contains what you type.
- **Deck** menu (when you have decks): **All cards** or one deck. With a deck, only its cards show, each with its count, for example "Fire Drake × 3".
- **Fronts**, **Backs** or **Both**.
- **Size**: a slider for how big the cards are.
- a count of the cards shown.

Click or tap a card to open it in the editor.

![The gallery with all cards in a grid](gallery.webp)

### Zooming and moving around

The gallery uses the same controls as the card editor:

- [[Ctrl]] + mouse wheel (⌘ on Mac), a pinch, or the − % + buttons in the bottom-right corner make the cards bigger or smaller.
- [[Ctrl+0]] resets the size.
- Hold [[Space]] or the middle mouse button and drag to move around. The normal scroll wheel and one-finger swipes scroll.

## Proofreading

The **Proofread** tab shows each card with all of its text, one box per field (for example Title, Rules text, Flavor text). Front fields come first; fields from the back are marked "(back)".

Your browser's spell check underlines spelling mistakes. Then:

- **Plain text** can be fixed right in the box. The change goes straight onto the card.
- **Formatted text** (with bold words, colours or other styling of its own) is marked "formatted" and can't be changed here. Click the card's name to open it, and fix it on the card.
- **Grey text** is the template's default text: that card has no text of its own in this field. Typing in the box gives the card its own text.

Search and the deck filter work here too, so you can proofread one deck at a time.

> **Tip:** To change the same word on many cards at once, use [Find and replace](help:find-replace) instead.

> **Note:** If nothing is underlined, check that spell check is turned on in your browser and set to your language.
