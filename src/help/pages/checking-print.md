# Checking the print

The PDF is made at exact millimetre sizes, but your printer settings can still shrink or move it. These checks take a few minutes and save a lot of card stock.

## Print at 100 percent

In your printer dialog, set the scale to **100%**, **Actual size** or **None**. Turn off options like **Fit to page**, **Shrink oversized pages** or **Scale to fit**. They make the whole page a little smaller, so the cards come out too small and the backs no longer line up.

Also check:

- the paper size in the printer dialog matches the paper you chose in the print view (A4 by default).
- the orientation is not being changed by the printer. Leave it on automatic, or match the print view.
- for double-sided printing, the flip (long or short edge) matches **Double-sided flip** in the print view.

## Measure a card

Print one sheet and measure it with a ruler:

1. Measure from one cut mark to the next, or along a guide line. This is the finished card. For an ID card it should be 54 × 86 mm (or 86 × 54 in landscape), the size shown in the print view under **Paper**.
2. Measure a whole row of cards. Small errors add up across a row, so a row of three 54 mm cards should be 162 mm when the cards touch.
3. If everything is a few percent too small, the printer scaled the page. Go back to the step above.

> **Tip:** A steel ruler is easier to read than a plastic one. Measure the longest distance you can for the best accuracy.

## Check the cut marks

- The marks should sit around the outside of the cards, with a small gap before the bleed starts. None should touch a card.
- When the marks run to the paper edge, the printer cuts them off at its own margin. That is normal.
- Lay a ruler from a mark on one side of the sheet to the matching mark on the other side. The line should run exactly along the edges of the cards.

If you use a corner cutter, turn on the corner cutter guide in the right pane to see in the preview how much of each corner will go. It is never printed.

## Check the backs against the light

For double-sided prints:

1. In the print view, press **Alignment test** (on a phone, **Test page**) and print it double-sided at 100%.
2. Hold the sheet up to a window or a lamp. The crosshairs on the front and back should sit on top of each other.
3. If they don't, read the distance on the small mm scales on the back and enter it as **Back offset X** / **Back offset Y**. Then print the test again.

You can do the same with a real sheet of cards: hold it to the light and check that the cut marks on both sides meet. See [Double-sided printing](help:double-sided#fixing-an-offset) for the details.

## Warnings after export

After **Export PDF**, the print view may list warnings:

- **A picture prints at about N DPI**: it will look blurry. Use a bigger picture, or make it smaller on the card. Around 300 DPI or more is best.
- **A font has no file**: that text prints as a 600 dpi picture instead of sharp letter shapes. Upload the font in the Fonts tab to fix it.
