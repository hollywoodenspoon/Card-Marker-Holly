# Layers, groups and alignment

Every text, picture, art box, icon row and shape on a card is a _layer_. The Layers list shows them all, top to bottom in the order they're stacked: the top of the list is in front.

## Where to find Layers

- **Computer**: at the top of the right pane. Click the **Layers** header to fold it away or open it again.
- **Tablet**: press **Open properties** at the right of the top bar.
- **Phone**: tap **Layers** in the bottom bar.

![The Layers list](layers.webp)

## Adding layers

Press **Add** in the top bar (on a phone, **Add** in the bottom bar) and pick what to add: text, a picture, an art box, an icon row, a card number or a shape. In Card mode the new layer belongs to this card only, as the menu says. In Template mode it becomes part of the template, on every card that uses it.

![The Add menu](add-menu.webp)

## What a row shows

- A **grip** (⋮⋮) on the left: drag it to move the layer up or down.
- An **icon** for the kind of layer, and its **name**.
- **field** after texts and art boxes whose content each card fills in.
- **if** when the part has a _Show only when…_ rule. See [Turning parts on and off](help:part-toggles).
- A letter such as **B** when the part reads a spreadsheet column in mail merge.
- **template** (in Card mode) for parts that come from the template.
- The **eye** to show or hide, and the **lock**.

## Select, rename and reorder

- Click a row to select that part on the card. [[Shift]]-click or [[Ctrl]]-click to select several.
- Double-click a name to rename it, or edit the name at the top of the properties (easier on touch). Clear names like _Rarity gem_ or _Flavor text_ help when you have many parts.
- Drag the grip to change the order. In **Layout** → **Arrange** you can also use **Bring to front**, **Bring forward**, **Send backward** and **Send to back**.

## Show, hide and lock

- **Eye**: in Template mode it sets whether the part shows by default. In Card mode it hides or shows the part **on this card only**; the other parts stay exactly where they are. A part whose eye is greyed out is always shown; the template decides that.
- **Lock**: a locked part can't be selected or moved on the canvas, so you can't knock it by accident. Click the lock again to unlock it.

## Card mode and Template mode

In **Template** mode the list shows the template's parts, and you can rearrange and edit all of them.

In **Card** mode it shows the template's parts plus anything you've added to this card only. The template's parts stay in their place in the stack; you can drag this card's own layers anywhere, also between the template's parts.

## Groups

Group parts that belong together, like a gem and its frame, so they move and resize as one.

1. Select two or more parts.
2. Press **Group selection** in the right pane, or [[Ctrl+G]] (⌘ on Mac).

To take a group apart again, select it and press [[Ctrl+Shift+G]].

A group shows in Layers with an arrow to fold or unfold it. Click a group on the card to select it, then click again on a part inside to select just that part.

## Align and distribute

The six buttons at the top of **Layout** align left, centre, right, top, middle and bottom.

- With **one** part selected, it aligns to the card.
- With **several** selected, they align to each other.

With three or more selected, **Distribute ↔** and **Distribute ↕** space them evenly across or down.

## Delete

Select a part and press [[Delete]] or [[Backspace]]. A message appears with **Undo** in case you didn't mean it. In Card mode you can't delete the template's parts; hide them on this card with the eye instead.
