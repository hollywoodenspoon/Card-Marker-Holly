# Find and replace

Find and replace changes a word or phrase on every card at once, for example when you rename a keyword from "Burn" to "Scorch". You see every match before anything changes, and one undo puts it all back.

## Opening it

- Press [[Ctrl+H]] (⌘ on Mac), or
- ☰ → **Find and replace…**

![The Find and replace dialog](find-replace.webp)

## Searching

Type in **Find**. The list below shows every place it occurs, as "card name · part name" and how many times, with the matches highlighted.

Options:

- **Match capitals**: "Fire" won't match "fire".
- **Whole words**: "fire" won't match "fireball".
- **Also template text**: also searches the default text on your templates (listed as "Template … · …").

The line at the bottom counts the matches and places that will change.

## Choosing what to replace

Every place starts ticked. Untick any you want to keep as they are. Click a place's name to open that card (or template) and look at it; this closes the dialog, so open it again to carry on.

## Replacing

1. Type the new text in **Replace with**. Leave it empty to delete the matches.
2. Press **Replace all**.
3. A message says how many were replaced.

If the result isn't what you wanted, press **Undo** next to the message, or later [[Ctrl+Z]] in the editor: everything goes back in one step.

The replacement keeps the formatting of the text it replaces, so a bold word stays bold.

## What it searches

- The text each card has typed in its own fields, on the front and the back.
- Text you added to a card only (in card mode).
- With **Also template text**: the template's default text, which shows on every card that hasn't typed its own.

> **Note:** A word whose letters have different formatting, such as half bold and half not, isn't found. Card numbers and symbols are searched as typed, e.g. `{fire}`, not as the picture they show.

> **Tip:** A card that still shows the template's default words hasn't got its own copy of them. To change those, tick **Also template text**; the change then shows on every card using that template.
