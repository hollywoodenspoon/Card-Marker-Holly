# About and credits

**Hearthside Cardworks Cozy Edition** is a card editor for designing and printing your own cards. It runs on your own computer, never goes online, and keeps everything on your own device: there are no accounts and no server.

The version you're using is shown at the bottom of the ☰ menu and on the Home screen.
