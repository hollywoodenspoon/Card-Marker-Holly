# Touch gestures

Hearthside Cardworks works with fingers, a pen or a mouse on every screen size. On touch screens the selection handles are bigger, every button is large enough to tap, and nothing depends on hovering.

## On the card

| Gesture                      | What it does                                  |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Tap a part                   | Select it                                     |
| Tap an empty spot            | Select nothing                                |
| Drag a part with one finger  | Move it                                       |
| Drag a corner or edge handle | Resize                                        |
| Drag the round handle        | Rotate (clicks into 45° steps near them)      |
| Double-tap text              | Edit the text                                 |
| Double-tap an art box        | Choose a picture, or reposition the one in it |
| Pinch                        | Zoom in and out                               |
| Drag with two fingers        | Pan                                           |

Putting a second finger down cancels a drag the first finger started, so you can always switch to zooming.

## Selecting several parts, duplicating and deleting

When something is selected on a touch screen, a small bar floats above the bottom of the canvas:

- **Select several** turns on adding: each tap adds a part to the selection, or takes it out again, and the bar counts them. Press **Done** to go back to normal taps. Move, align or group them as with one part.
- **Duplicate** and **Delete** (the copy and bin buttons) act on the selected parts. They show for parts you can change here: a card's own layers, or template parts in **Template** mode. After a delete, the message at the bottom has **Undo**.

Pictures keep their proportions when you resize them by touch. For an exact or stretched size, type **Width** and **Height** in the **Layout** section of the **Edit** sheet.

## Editing text on a phone

Double-tap a text and a panel opens at the top of the screen with the text tools and a bigger copy of the text to type in. The card below updates as you type. Tap **Done** when you've finished.

## Panels and sheets

- **Phone**: tap **Cards**, **Layers**, **Add**, **Assets** or **Edit** in the bottom bar to open a sheet. Close it with ✕, by tapping the same button again, or by swiping its header down.
- The **Edit** sheet stays low, so the card stays visible and tappable above it.
- **Tablet**: tap **Open panels** (left of the top bar) or **Open properties** (right) to slide out a drawer.

## Lists

- To reorder **Cards**, **Layers** or the print list, drag the grip (⋮⋮) at the left of a row.
- Images can't be dragged into an asset folder by touch. Use the image's ⋯ → **Move to folder…** instead.
- To change many cards at once, press **Select** in **Cards** and tick the cards.

## Print preview

| Gesture               | What it does |
| --------------------- | ------------ |
| Pinch                 | Zoom         |
| Drag with two fingers | Pan          |

The − / % / + buttons at the bottom right zoom too, and the percentage button fits the sheet again. On a phone the preview shows one side at a time; tap **Front** or **Back** next to the sheet number below it to switch.

## Gallery

| Gesture           | What it does                     |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Swipe up and down | Scroll through the cards         |
| Pinch             | Make the cards bigger or smaller |
| Tap a card        | Open it in the editor            |
