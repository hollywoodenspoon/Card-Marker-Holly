# Adventurer Scuffle! (Open Skirmish)

A test mode: the Mutual Reveal Skirmish is replaced by an open board and actions. Combat is unchanged; at Regroup every field card goes to Support. Fate Numbers are not used.

## Overview

Two players each bring a Free Company of 12 cards. The game is played in rounds, and each round has two Engagements worth 1 point each. The first player to reach 4 points wins immediately.

**Components:** each player's 12 cards, a coin, and 2 cubes to mark each player's Main Attacker.

## Building a Free Company

- Exactly 12 cards, one copy of each.
- At most one Ace.

New players can use a premade Free Company: Adventurer's Guild, Hero's Retinue or Necromancer Deck.

## Start of the Game

1. Play rock-paper-scissors. The winner chooses who has the initiative.
2. Each player places 6 of their 12 cards face down on their field. The initiative player places first. The other 6 cards are that player's Support.

**The field** has 6 positions in 2 rows and 3 columns:

- The top row is the Front Line and the bottom row is the Rear Guard.
- The middle column is the Center and the two side columns are the Flank.

## Round Structure

Each round has a Skirmish Phase, a Combat Phase and a Regroup Phase. Cards placed at the start and refilled at Regroup go face down and are revealed at the start of the next Skirmish.

### Skirmish Phase

**Reveal**

1. Both players turn all their face-down field cards face up at the same time.
2. The initiative player picks one of their Flip Abilities and resolves it.
3. The other player picks one of theirs and resolves it.
4. Keep alternating until every Flip Ability has resolved. When one player has none left, the other resolves the rest of theirs in any order.

Flip Abilities are mandatory, and the card's owner picks the targets. With no legal target, nothing happens. A Flip Ability does not make its card ability-active. A face-down card that was made ability-active does not trigger its Flip Ability.

**Actions**

Starting with the initiative player, players take turns doing one action each:

- **Attack:** turn a face-up Ready card clockwise. Up to 3 cards a round. The last card you turn to attack is your Main Attacker. Mark it with a cube.
- **Defense:** turn a face-up Ready card counter-clockwise. Up to 3 cards a round.
- **Field Ability:** use the Field Ability of a face-up Ready card. The card becomes ability-active.
- **Support Ability:** use a Support Ability from your Support.
- **Pass:** you take no more actions this round. The other player keeps going until they pass too.

Attaching a Living Weapon (such as Living Weapon Dusk from the rest zone) is your action for the turn.

Howling Barbarian does not count toward your 3 attackers. If it is still Ready when both players pass, it attacks.

When both players have passed, form the stacks:

1. Your attack stack is your Main Attacker plus your attackers adjacent to it.
2. It attacks the Mirrored Position of your Main Attacker on the opponent's field.
3. The opponent's defense stack is their defenders on or adjacent to that position.
4. Unbound cards join stacks from anywhere.
5. Values lock. Every card's Attack and Defense, including position bonuses, stays fixed for the rest of the round.

The Main Attacker only decides where the attack comes from and what it targets.

### Combat Phase

1. Each player's attack is one Engagement. The initiative player chooses which one resolves first. That is the First Engagement, and the other is the Second Engagement.
    - A player with no attacker has no Engagement this round. The other player's Engagement is the First Engagement.
    - If an attack has no defender on or adjacent to its target, the attacker scores the point immediately.
2. Starting totals: the attack stack's total Attack against the defense stack's total Defense.
3. Fighting Spirit: players take turns revealing one card at a time. The initiative player starts in every Engagement.
    - On your turn, reveal a card from your Support and add its Fighting Spirit to your side. Alternatively, use a rest-zone card whose Rest Ability allows it. That card stays in the rest zone and can be used once per Engagement.
    - Resolve the card's Battle Ability before the next reveal.
    - Instead of revealing, you may pass. Passing locks your total for this Engagement. The other player may keep revealing until they also pass.
    - If a card moves between your attack stack and your defense stack, totals update immediately. Each card keeps its locked values.
4. Scoring: the higher total scores 1 point. A tie scores nothing. Reaching 4 points wins immediately.
5. Between Engagements, players may use any Rest Ability whose condition is met.
6. Resolve the Second Engagement the same way. Unused Support cards carry over to it.

### Regroup Phase

1. Rest every card used for Fighting Spirit, every unused Support card and every attached Living Weapon.
2. Every field card goes to your Support, Ready cards included, unless an ability keeps it on the field.
3. Refill every empty field position face down from your rest zone, choosing which card goes where. The initiative player refills first. If your rest zone runs out, fill the rest from your Support.
4. Pass the initiative to the other player.

## Terminology

- **Free Company:** a player's 12-card deck.
- **Support:** the cards in a player's hand.
- **Rest / Rest Zone:** to rest a card is to put it in your rest zone, a face-up pile.
- **Ready:** a card that has not attacked, defended or used an ability this round.
- **Active:** a card that is attacking (turned clockwise), defending (turned counter-clockwise) or ability-active (turned diagonally).
- **Adjacent:** directly above, below, left or right. Diagonals do not count.
- **Mirrored Position:** the same row and the opposite column on the opponent's field.
- **Won / Lost an Engagement:** you scored its point, or the other player did. A tie is neither.
- **Attack, Defense and Fighting Spirit:** printed on the left, right and top edges of the card. Grey icons show the most a stat can reach through the card's own ability.
- **Swapped-in card:** a card an ability puts on the field in place of another. It takes the replaced card's face and state unless the ability says otherwise.

## Ability Types

- **Field Ability:** used by a face-up Ready card as an action. The card becomes ability-active.
- **Support Ability:** used from your Support as an action. Show the card, resolve it, then rest it unless the text says otherwise.
- **Rest Ability:** used from the rest zone when its condition is met.
- **Battle Ability:** resolves when its card is revealed as Fighting Spirit. Its condition is checked once, at reveal.
- **Inherent Ability:** always on. Checks that look at the field only count face-up cards.
- **Flip Ability:** resolves in the Reveal at the start of the Skirmish, in turns.

## Keywords

- **Center / Flank:** only while the card is in that zone.
- **Initiative / No-Initiative:** whether you have the initiative this round.
- **Attacking Side / Defending Side:** your side in the current Engagement.
- **Won First Engagement / Lost Previous Engagement:** only the current round counts.
- **3+ Rest Zone:** your rest zone holds 3 or more cards.
- **Rest 1:** rest 1 card of your choice from your Support to use the ability.
- **Attack Unbound:** all your attackers count for your Attack.
- **Defense Unbound:** all your defenders count for your Defense.
- **Archetypes (Adventuring, Rianz, Grave, Ghoulish):** a card belongs to an archetype if its name contains the word. An archetype check never counts the card itself.
- **"Opponent rests a card from their Support":** the opponent chooses the card.
- **"Cannot be repositioned":** protects against every swap by an opponent's ability.

### Living Weapon

- Attaching puts the weapon under a ready card on your field and adds its Attack and Defense to that card.
- A card can hold one weapon at a time.
- If the host leaves the field, the weapon goes to the rest zone.
- A weapon on an ability-active host does nothing.
